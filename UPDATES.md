# 🚀 The Japa Desk — Future Product Roadmap & Potential Upgrades

Welcome to **The Japa Desk** (formerly Application OS) — the all-in-one platform for international students and immigrants navigating university applications, scholarships, document verification, FX capital, and visa pathways.

This document outlines high-impact potential upgrades, AI feature additions, and strategic enhancements to guide future development iterations.

---

## 🔮 Phase 1: AI & Automation Enhancements

### 📄 1. AI Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Essay Feedback Agent
- **Feature**: Upload draft SOPs or Motivation Letters directly in the Vault or Requirements Checklist.
- **Functionality**: An AI agent evaluates the essay against university-specific rubrics (structure, academic intent, tone, grammar, and alignment with faculty research).
- **Output**: Generates inline suggestions, readability scores, and structural improvements.

### 🔍 2. Automated PDF OCR & Document Data Extraction
- **Feature**: Auto-extract metadata from uploaded PDF documents.
- **Functionality**: When an applicant uploads a passport or IELTS transcript to the Document Vault, OCR automatically extracts:
  - Expiry Date (automatically setting the 90-day alert)
  - Document/Passport Number
  - Exam Scores (Overall Band, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking)

### 🎯 3. Smart University & Scholarship Matchmaker
- **Feature**: AI-powered recommendation engine based on user profile.
- **Functionality**: Evaluates the user's Academic Profile (GPA, YOE, Degree, Target Intake, and Budget) against a database of global universities and scholarships to recommend:
  - Reach / Target / Safety universities
  - Fully funded vs. partial tuition waiver scholarships

---

## 🏛️ Phase 2: Relocation & Visa Operations ("Japa" Module)

### 💰 4. Proof of Funds (POF) & Blocked Account Simulator
- **Feature**: Automated country-specific living expense & POF calculator.
- **Functionality**:
  - **Germany**: Blocked Account calculation (€11,904/year)
  - **UK**: Maintenance Funds (£1,334/month in London, £1,023/month outside)
  - **Canada**: Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) ($20,635 CAD)
- **Integration**: Compares required POF directly against liquid capital recorded in the FX Capital Ledger.

### 📅 5. Visa Embassy Appointment & Slot Tracker
- **Feature**: Real-time tracking for visa interview appointment slots (VFS Global, TLScontact, US Embassy).
- **Functionality**: Sends browser notifications or SMS/Email alerts when new interview appointment dates open up for target embassies.

### ✈️ 6. Pre-Departure & Flight / Housing Checklist
- **Feature**: Relocation phase operational tracker.
- **Functionality**:
  - Flight booking comparison & flight budget logger
  - Student health insurance verification (e.g. TK, Barmer, OSHC, IHS)
  - Temporary housing & dorm deposit tracker

---

## 💳 Phase 3: Financial & Banking Integrations

### 🏦 7. Open Banking Wallet Sync (Mono / Stitch / Plaid)
- **Feature**: Automated liquid capital balance updates.
- **Functionality**: Connect local bank accounts or FX wallets (e.g. Wise, Revolut, Grey, Pyypl) to automatically sync liquid balances into the Capital Ledger without manual entry.

### 💱 8. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) FX Hedging & Tuition Payment Calculator
- **Feature**: Real-time tuition payment currency hedging tool.
- **Functionality**: Calculates expected tuition costs across fluctuating local exchange rates and sends alerts when local rates hit favorable conversion windows.

---

## 👥 Phase 4: Collaboration & Community Features

### 👨‍👩‍👧 9. Advisor / Sponsor / Family Portal
- **Feature**: Shareable applicant dashboard link.
- **Functionality**: Generate secure read-only or commentable share links for parents, financial sponsors, or education consultants to track application status in real-time.

### 📊 10. Anonymized Acceptance Data & Outcome Insights
- **Feature**: Crowd-sourced admission decision database.
- **Functionality**: Applicants can opt-in to contribute anonymized GPA, GRE/IELTS, and acceptance outcomes, allowing users to benchmark their chances for specific programs.
